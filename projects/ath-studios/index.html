PROJECT 01 · AI & AUTOMATION

Hiring & HR, automated end to end.

A live n8n automation portfolio that connects job applications, AI powered CV evaluation, candidate coaching and policy grounded HR assistance into one workflow ecosystem.

Launch application ↗View source on GitHub ↗

Overview

ATH Studios demonstrates how repetitive recruitment and HR tasks can be coordinated through n8n. Candidates can submit a PDF CV, receive an AI-generated coaching report and interact with a policy assistant, while HR teams get structured candidate evaluations and operational alerts.

The problem

Recruitment workflows often involve disconnected forms, manual CV reviews, repeated candidate questions and limited visibility when an automation fails. This project brings those activities into a connected workflow system with traceable outputs.

Core capabilities

  • Job application intake: validates candidate data, stores CV files in Google Drive and logs applications in Google Sheets.
  • AI CV review: compares a CV with a job post and produces a match score, strengths, gaps and suggested keywords.
  • Candidate coaching: generates cover letter guidance and likely interview questions.
  • AI screening: scores new applications and routes decisions for interview, HR review or decline.
  • Policy-grounded HR chat: answers questions using company policy documents loaded into a knowledge base.
  • Error monitoring: classifies failures, sends alerts and checks for stalled applications.

Workflow architecture

Website forms and chat connect to n8n through a fixed HTTPS endpoint. n8n orchestrates the workflows and communicates with Google services and Gemini. Credentials remain inside n8n rather than being exposed in the browser.

Implementation notes

The public repository includes six importable workflows. The project documentation explains the required credentials, placeholder IDs, Google Sheets structure and activation steps. Live functionality depends on the configured n8n host and its availability.

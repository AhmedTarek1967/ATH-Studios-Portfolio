PROJECT 01 · AI & AUTOMATION

Hiring & HR, automated end to end.

Apply with a PDF CV, get every candidate scored by AI, send a personal coaching report, answer policy questions from company documents and never miss a failed run. Six n8n workflows working as one system.

Launch application ↗View source on GitHub ↗

Overview

HiringHR shows how repetitive recruitment and HR tasks can be coordinated through n8n. Every button on its landing page runs a real workflow: candidates submit a PDF CV, receive an AI-generated coaching report and ask a policy assistant questions, while HR teams get scored candidate evaluations and operational alerts.

The problem

Recruitment workflows often involve disconnected forms, manual CV reviews, repeated candidate questions and limited visibility when an automation fails. This project brings those activities into a connected workflow system with traceable outputs.

Core capabilities

  • Job application intake: validates the application, blocks duplicates, saves the CV to Google Drive and logs a row in Google Sheets.
  • AI CV review: four AI agents compare a CV with a job post and email a match score, strengths, gaps and keywords.
  • Candidate coaching: writes a cover letter from the CV and prepares five likely interview questions with model answers.
  • AI screening: scores every new application out of 100, then sends an interview invite, flags it to HR or sends a polite decline.
  • Policy-grounded HR chat: answers only from the company policy documents loaded into a knowledge base.
  • Error monitoring: classifies failures, sends alerts, emails a daily digest and runs a heartbeat that catches stalled applications.

Workflow architecture

Website forms and chat connect to n8n through a fixed HTTPS endpoint. n8n orchestrates the workflows and communicates with Google services and Gemini. Credentials remain inside n8n rather than being exposed in the browser.

Implementation notes

The public repository includes six importable workflows: Applying to Job, Cv Review, Ai Action, Rag, Policy Loader and Error Handeling. The README explains the required credentials, placeholder IDs, Google Sheets columns and activation steps. The knowledge base is held in memory, so Policy Loader refreshes it on every start. Live functionality depends on the configured n8n host and its availability.
